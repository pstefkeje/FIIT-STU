# FIIT-STU-MIP- 2026/2027
title: Enginnering education platform for secondary schools
acronym:EngEPSS
annotation :Project Proposal: Engineering education platform for secondary schools under
the auspices of the Ministry of Education
1. Core Features Content - Education Directory: Secondary/post-secondary
electrical schools and admissions guide.
- Certification Exams: Information and accredited institutions for qualification
exams.
- Institution Directory: Verified list of exam centers with links, addresses, and
contacts.
- Materials Content: 1) Video Tutorials: Restricted-access courses for creden-
tial renewal/upgrading. 2) Exercises Gamified Learning: Interactive tasks and
games sorted by difficulty.
- Legislation: Up-to-date electrical safety standards and regulations.
- Internships: Practical training connections with institutions and employers.
- Job Board: Current job openings in electrical engineering.
- Extracurriculars: After-school technical clubs for students.
- Summer Camps: Specialized technical camps for elementary and high school
pupils.
- Inspection Courses: Training dates for prospective inspection technicians via
Technick´a inˇspekcia, a.s.
2. Educational Structure - Professional Courses: Scheduled in-person train-
ing with mandatory attendance for qualified adults.
- School Admissions: Information on deadlines and entry requirements for sec-
ondary and distance study.
- On-Site Exams: Qualification testing (§21, §22) held at authorized schools.
- Inspection Training: Advanced certification organized with Technick´a inˇspekcia,
a.s.
- On-Site Equipment: All components, tools, and wiring provided on-site.
- Video Archives: Personalized online access to recorded practical sessions for
each group.
- Youth Engagement: Gamified learning, clubs, and extracurriculars managed
by instructors.
3. Mission Funding - Target Audience: Elementary/secondary pupils, uni-
versity students, and adult learners.
- Goals: Provide full-spectrum electrical education, retraining, and job place-
ment.
- Funding: Ministry of Education grants, tech sponsors, and employer contri-
butions.
